02 / DIAGNOSE

A baseline you can inspect

The dashboard uses a transparent vegetation condition index and historical baseline. Its index is a proxy for vegetation condition, not forest survival, remaining area, or a calibrated probability.

Candidate drivers are associations supported by available observations. More than one driver may be plausible; limited evidence can leave the cause uncertain.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Early warning, with limits

Autocorrelation and variance can help investigate resilience. They also respond to seasonality, measurement noise, trends, missing data, and changing external conditions.

Insufficient or irregular histories produce an unavailable result. No output establishes an irreversible tipping point or a time to ecosystem collapse.

04 / EVALUATE

Research before claims

Research models can learn from satellite images, radar, climate, and changes over time. Connected analysis prepares the available observations and checks whether an installed model can use them. Saved model predictions appear in Model runs.

A model needs compatible observations and confirmed reference outcomes for training. Its accuracy must then be tested on different places and later dates, using independent evidence.

Five-year sensitivity scenarios retain explicit hypothetical assumptions. Ecological predictive skill and intervention effects require independent validation. Training on demonstration data checks the software only.
